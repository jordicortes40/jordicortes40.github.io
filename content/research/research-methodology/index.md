## Overview

Research methodology is a central component of my work. I investigate how statistical methods are applied, reported and interpreted, with the aim of improving the quality, transparency and reproducibility of scientific research.

My research combines methodological developments with meta-research studies that evaluate current research practices and identify opportunities to improve statistical reporting and study design.

## Research Interests

- Reporting guidelines
- Meta-research
- Reproducible research
- Statistical reporting
- Study design and methodology
- Research quality assessment

## Current Research

A major focus of my recent work is the evaluation of statistical reporting in biomedical and sports science research. By systematically assessing published studies, I aim to identify common methodological limitations and promote best practices for transparent and reproducible research.

I am also interested in developing practical recommendations and methodological tools that help researchers design, analyse and report studies more effectively.

## Impact

Improving research methodology ultimately contributes to more reliable scientific evidence, better decision-making and greater confidence in research findings. My goal is to bridge methodological research with practical guidance that supports researchers across different scientific disciplines.
